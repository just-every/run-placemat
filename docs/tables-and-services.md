Before guests can book, Placemat needs your room and a service: the tables you have, and the hours, turn length and capacity of a sitting such as Friday dinner. Owners and managers set these up under **Services & tables**.

## Add your tables

Expand **Add table** and fill in:

- **Table label**, the name staff use, such as `T1`.
- **Seats**, how many people the table holds.
- **Across the room (%)** and **Down the room (%)**, where the table sits on the floor plan. 0% is the left or top wall and 100% the right or bottom wall.

Press **Save table**. The **Tables** list shows each table's seats and position, and the floor plan on **Floor & bookings** draws it at that spot.

Placemat combines tables by itself when one table cannot fit a party, so add each physical table on its own. A table that already has bookings shows **History retained** and can no longer be edited, so the history stays true; when your layout changes, add a new table.

## Create a service

Expand **Create service** and fill in:

- **Service name**, for example `Friday dinner`.
- **Turn duration (minutes)**, how long a party keeps its table, for example 90.
- **Starts** and **Ends**, in the venue's timezone.
- **Cover limit**, the most guests you will take across the whole service.

Press **Save service**. The service is a **draft**: it is private, and guests cannot book it yet. You can still change it with **Edit service**.

## Publish it

Press **Prepare publication** on the draft. Placemat records the exact service, its hours, capacity and table plan as a plan to approve, and takes you to **Approvals & reminders**.

Expand **Exact plan** to read what will be published, then press **Approve plan**. The service is now **published**: it appears on the guest booking page, and staff can seat walk-ins and keep a waitlist for it.

Publication fixes the service's hours, turn duration and cover limit. Editing a draft after preparing it makes the old plan stale; prepare a new one. To offer another sitting, create another service.
