# Verifying a restaurant installation

CONTRACT.md is the source of acceptance text and status. ACCEPTANCE.md and docs/CLAIMS.md are generated views; the carried-done trace is [DONE-AUDIT.md](contract/DONE-AUDIT.md).

Use isolated installations with realistic restaurant and guest records. Exercise screens, public HTTP doors, downloads, commands and authenticated provider callbacks. Paid and network boundaries may use provider sandboxes or official-schema fakes; native capacity, role and record actions run through Placemat itself.

Record each acceptance outcome and its actual assertion, not a title or an HTTP success alone. Reconciliation needs all named terms; an absence needs populated records and an assertion of that absence. A no-write preview is checked before committing its action. Refusals read the affected records and outbound effects back.

Browser journeys use the Base accessibility bar at 1440 and 390. Field qualifications remain visible in the contract register; they do not replace native fixtures. Historical receipts are in CHANGELOG.md and do not establish success on a later commit.
