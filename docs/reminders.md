Placemat prepares reminder messages and calendar files; you send them with the tools you already use. Placemat itself sends no email or SMS to guests.

## Reminder messages

Under **Approvals & reminders**, press **Prepare reminder batch** for the selected service. Placemat collects the bookings that are still booked and have not had a reminder, and records the batch as a plan.

Choose English or Spanish before preparing the batch. Read **Exact reminder previews** to check each recipient and the exact text, template version and recorded policy versions, then press **Approve plan**. A changed booking, contact, template or applicable terms requires a fresh batch and review. **Approved reminders** then counts the reminders ready to export.

Press **Download reminders CSV**. The file holds each guest's contact details and the approved message, ready for your email or SMS tool. Cancelled and no-show bookings, and erased guests, are left out. **Reminder messages** lists every approved message and when it was approved.

## Reminder templates

Under **Settings**, expand **Reminder templates** to edit English and Spanish text. Saving appends a version; it never changes previously approved messages. Keep the venue, date, party size and cancellation terms substitutions. The listed substitutions insert plain text once, so a guest name containing markup or another substitution stays literal. Previews escape markup; CSV downloads escape quotes and spreadsheet formulas. Dates use US conventions in the venue timezone; US phone substitutions include the area code.

Recorded applicable fee and experience cancellation terms are copied exactly, with their original policy versions. Placemat does not translate policy terms or invent missing terms, fee consent or sending permission. A financially implicated booking without recorded cancellation terms cannot produce a reminder proposal. No recorded policy produces no invented cancellation promise. Review the actual terms language before using your own sender.

The reminders CSV includes language, template identity and version, policy references and exact cancellation terms. The business exports include all saved template revisions and reminder metadata; structured backups preserve them. Guest erasure clears linked contact and rendered-message copies from reminders, proposals, audits and subsequent backups. Existing backup erasure fences prevent restoring earlier personal values.

Actual provider delivery, channel preferences, translation and unusable-contact tasks, and other communication templates remain unimplemented in this slice. Template editing and reminder approval authorize preparation and export only.

## Calendar files

Each booking in the bookings list has a **Calendar** link that downloads it as a calendar file (`.ics`), with its start and end time, for your own calendar or to send to the guest.
