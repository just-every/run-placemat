# Private reservation management

The venue owner or a native admin manager with reservation authority explicitly sends a transactional email from the existing production mail configuration. The recipient is the reservation's recorded contact; the command accepts no destination override. The response reports provider acceptance, never mailbox delivery, and contains no management bearer. Development and demo installations refuse this outbound action.

The email carries `/manage-booking#token=…`. The fragment is removed from browser history as the page opens, is never sent in the document request, and is held only in page memory. Requests post it to the same origin. A random 32-byte capability addresses exactly one reservation. Only its SHA-256 digest is stored. It expires at the earlier of 24 hours after issue and service start. An explicit owner or manager reissue invalidates its predecessor. Every issue is audited with the actor, and at most five issues (including reissues or failed-delivery attempts) are allowed per reservation per venue-local calendar day. Hosts, shared-device sessions and agents cannot issue. This door sends by email only; any future SMS door needs MSG-008 consent. A successful change, reconfirmation or cancellation rotates the bearer without extending that expiry. Failed availability checks leave both reservation and link intact.

Every write requires an exact saved preview and an affirmative acknowledgment of its fee/refund consequences. A changed reservation, stale preview, expired token or replay cannot authorize another reservation. D1 guards bind the current reservation, guest, contact, token and preview to the inventory update and rotation. Existing area/access constraints, physical inventory, staffing and locked assignments remain binding.

Contact edits belong to one reservation and do not rewrite the guest profile or another reservation. Changing the recorded email invalidates access entirely, returning no replacement bearer. The guest must ask the venue to explicitly issue a new email link to the updated address; no email is sent automatically. Staff can read the reservation contact; booking CSVs and validated archives preserve it. Guest erasure removes the contact and its history. Bearer records are excluded from archives; restoring a venue requires the owner to issue new links.

The current zero-money path discloses a zero fee and zero refund. Any recorded payment request, captured charge or refund requires manager review before guest mutation. PAY-004/PAY-005 retain fee/refund policy and execution obligations; no payment is taken and no refund is automatically submitted here.
